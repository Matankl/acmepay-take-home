**Sarah (2026-05-24 11:45):**
Hi! Sorry you're dealing with this. The good news on a fraud-code dispute is you usually have decent evidence available — IP address, device fingerprint, and any prior orders from the same customer.

You have 7 calendar days from when the dispute was filed to submit your response through the dashboard. You can find it under Disputes > D-502. Upload anything you have: order receipt, delivery confirmation, customer email exchange, etc. If you don't respond by the deadline you lose by default.

One thing to note — even if you win, the $15 chargeback fee isn't refunded, and the disputed $85 is debited from your balance now and will return ~60-75 days after the case is resolved (if you win).

Let me know if you want help drafting the evidence narrative — happy to look at what you've got.
