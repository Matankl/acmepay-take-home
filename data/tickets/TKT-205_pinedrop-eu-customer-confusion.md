**Merchant (2026-05-14 11:00):**
quick question — a few of our UK and DE customers have been emailing customer service complaining about being charged "international fees" on top of the product price. we're not charging them anything extra, the price is the price. is acmepay adding something on the customer side?

**Sarah (2026-05-14 12:15):**
Nothing on our side. The 1.5% international card surcharge is charged to *you* (the merchant), not to the cardholder. So if a UK customer buys a $100 product, they're charged $100 and you net $93.60 instead of $96.10. Their bank may show foreign-transaction fees from their own issuer (totally separate from us — that's between them and their bank), or currency conversion if they're shopping in a non-home currency.

Suggestion: in customer comms, you can confidently say "we don't charge any international fees" because you don't. Their issuer might. Hope that helps clear it up.
