The conditions where we'd revisit:

- account on platform 12+ months with no risk events
- volume stability month-over-month

I'd suggest reaching out to your account manager (cc'd) to discuss whether any of these are within reach. They may also be able to talk about partial relief like reducing the rolling reserve % even if the T+5 window stays.
